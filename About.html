About Lee Baw Seng

Scaffolding contractor in Singapore, with roots in traditional Chinese opera stage building.

Our story

Lee Beng Seng’s father learnt to build Chinese opera stages from master craftsmen from Fujian: wooden poles, a few nails, and rope lashings strong enough to hold 400 kg. Beng Seng started helping him at 13 or 14, and father and son built opera stages together for about 40 years.

In the 1980s, the busiest years, they built at least 400 stages a year. From around 2000, metal stages took over and wooden ones became rare, so the family moved into scaffolding and kept stage building as side work.

After their father passed away, the brothers Beng Seng and Terry took over the business, and scaffolding rental is now our main work.
